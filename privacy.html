Privacy Policy

Pocket Personal (lethanhdong.one) — Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy sets out how we handle the personal data you provide when you use Pocket Personal — a personal suite of tools for English learning, stock tracking, voice, chess and music (“Services”). For the purposes of this policy, ‘we’, ‘us’ and ‘our’ refers to the operator of Pocket Personal, and ‘you’ refers to you, the user of the Services.

1.1. Privacy Principles

We follow two fundamental principles when it comes to your private data:

  • We don't use your data to show you ads, and we never sell your data.
  • We only keep the data the Services need in order to work.

1.2. Terms of Service

This Privacy Policy forms part of our Terms of Service, which describes the terms under which you use our Services. This Privacy Policy should therefore be read in conjunction with those terms.

2. Data We Collect

Account information. When you register, we collect your email address and a password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name and profile picture from Google.

Content you create. Sentences you save, texts you upload for reading, your stock watchlists and portfolios, price alerts, and your app settings. This content exists only so the Services can show it back to you on your devices.

Usage counts. We count things like how many words you look up or how much text you convert to speech each day. These counts exist only to enforce the usage limits of free and paid plans.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and identify your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store the content you create and sync it across your devices.
  • To enforce the usage limits of your plan.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising. Some features ask an outside service to process something you choose to send — for example, the text you choose to convert into speech, or the words you choose to translate. Only the content you explicitly submit for that feature is sent, and only to perform that feature.

5. Data Security

Your password is never stored in a readable form. Your sentence bank is encrypted in a way that only you can unlock with your password — we cannot read it ourselves. If you forget your password, your encrypted sentences cannot be recovered. All communication between your device and our servers is encrypted.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You may ask us to delete your account and everything associated with it at any time — just contact us (see below). Deleting your account removes your profile, your saved content and your sign-in sessions.

7. Children's Privacy

The Services include a kid mode for users under 18, to be used under the supervision of the account holder. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children without the account holder's involvement.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
